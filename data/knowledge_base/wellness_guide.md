# LifeFlow Extended Knowledge Base

## Indian Superfoods
- **Moringa (Drumstick leaves)**: Highest plant-based calcium, iron, vitamin C.
- **Flaxseeds**: Best plant omega-3 source. 1 tbsp daily = 1.5g ALA.
- **Amla (Indian Gooseberry)**: 10× more vitamin C than oranges. Supports immunity.
- **Turmeric + Black Pepper**: Curcumin absorption increases 2000% with piperine.
- **Kokum**: Anti-inflammatory, excellent for gut health.

## Meal Timing for Metabolic Health
- **Breakfast within 1 hour of waking**: Kickstarts metabolism.
- **Lunch 12-2 PM**: Largest meal; digestive fire is highest at midday.
- **Dinner before 7:30 PM**: 10-12 hour overnight fast improves insulin sensitivity.
- **Post-workout**: 20g protein within 30 min post-exercise for muscle recovery.

## Hydration Guide
- **Target**: 35 ml per kg body weight per day.
- For 60 kg person: ~2.1 L/day.
- Signs of dehydration: dark yellow urine, headache, low energy.
- Electrolytes: add pinch of Himalayan salt + lemon to water after sweating.

## Mental Health & Productivity
- **Pomodoro Technique**: 25 min focus + 5 min break → 90 min cycle.
- **Digital detox**: 2 hours screen-free daily reduces anxiety by 30%.
- **Nature exposure**: 20 min in green space significantly reduces cortisol.
- **Journaling**: 5-minute gratitude practice improves life satisfaction.

## Exercise Science Basics
- **Zone 2 cardio** (comfortable jog/walk): Best for fat oxidation and longevity.
- **Strength training 2×/week**: Prevents muscle loss, improves bone density.
- **Stretching**: 10 min flexibility work reduces injury risk by 60%.
- **Step count**: 7,000-8,000 steps/day associated with longevity benefits.
